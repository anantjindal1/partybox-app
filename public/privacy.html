Privacy Policy

PartyBox · Effective 20 September 2026

The short version. PartyBox needs no account. You pick a name and an avatar; we tie your progress to a random ID stored on your device. We measure anonymous usage to improve the games. Google Analytics and ads run only if you accept them. You can delete everything we hold about your device from your Profile page. We do not sell your data.

1. Who we are

PartyBox is a collection of multiplayer party and card games, available as a website and as an app. Questions about this policy or your data: message us through the feedback link (WhatsApp).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, email address, phone number, or payment details. The microphone is used only while you hold the talk button, and only after your browser or phone asks your permission.

3. Consent and your choices

  • Google Analytics and ads are off until you accept. Declining keeps every game fully playable.
  • You can change your mind any time: use "Analytics & ads choice" at the bottom of the home screen or in Profile.
  • Basic usage records (Section 2) support running and improving the service. You can erase them with "Delete my data".
  • Denying microphone permission only disables voice.

4. Who else sees your data

  • Other players in your room see your name, avatar, moves, reactions, anything you type into a game, and your voice clips. Room data is not private to individual players: treat it as visible to anyone who has the room code. Don't put personal information in names, confessions or voice.
  • Google (Firebase / Cloud Firestore) hosts our database. Data may be processed on servers outside India.
  • Vercel hosts the website and sees standard request data such as your IP address.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase, only if you accept.
  • Google AdSense (or another ad network we may add), only if you accept and ads are switched on. Ad providers may use identifiers or cookies to choose and measure ads.

Fonts are hosted by us; no font requests go to Google. We do not sell personal data.

5. Retention

  • Rooms (players, game state, actions, reactions, voice clips) are set to be deleted automatically about 24 hours after they are created. Deletion by our database provider can lag by a day or two.
  • Profile, progress and usage records are kept until you delete them with "Delete my data", or we remove them.
  • On your device the data stays until you clear your browser or app storage or use "Delete my data".

6. Deleting your data

Open Profile → Privacy → Delete my data. This erases your synced profile, XP, per-game stats, usage records, and everything stored on that device. Because we identify you only by that random device ID, we cannot look up your data by name or by any other detail. If you can't reach the Profile page, message us and we will help. Rooms you were in disappear on their own as described above.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before you withdrew it.

8. Children

PartyBox is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. If you are under 18, please use PartyBox with the knowledge and permission of a parent or guardian.

9. Security

Connections to our servers use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. Please don't share sensitive information in any game.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top and, where the change matters to your consent, ask again.
