## Full description (max 4000)
PartyBox brings the games you play at every family get-together, hostel night and office party into one app. No sign-up, no downloads for your friends: you host a room, share the link on WhatsApp, and everyone joins from their own phone, in the app or straight from the browser.

CARD GAMES
Bluff, Bhabhi (Get Away), Call Break, Judgement (Kachuful), Court Piece (Rang), Satti (Sevens), Dassi Pakad (Mendikot), 3-2-5 (Teen Do Paanch), Teri and Donkey (Gadha). Real rules, partner play where the game has it, and a full card table on every phone.

PARTY & SOCIAL
Dumb Charades with Bollywood movies and songs, Sabse Zyada Kaun, Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi, Bhed (Jasoos), Bakwaas, Bakwaas Adaalat, Chugli Detective and Kodename.

QUIZ & CLASSIC
ThinkFast solo trivia, FirstBell live quiz battles, and Tambola.

WHY PARTYBOX
• Friends don't need the app: they join with a room code or link from any browser
• Play in the same room or across cities
• English and Hindi
• Open Tables: find a game with a free seat right now
• Voice clips and emoji reactions to keep the banter going
• Free to play

PartyBox is for friendly games. No real money, no betting.
